Parish Life

Our vibrant community organizations

KCYM Ayroor
@kcymayroor

Kerala Catholic Youth Movement – Empowering the youth of our parish through spiritual formation, social service, and cultural activities. The vibrant youth wing organizes retreats, skill workshops, and community outreach programs.

Follow on Instagram
CLC Ayroor
@clc.ayroor

Christian Life Community – A fellowship of young adults dedicated to living out their faith through prayer, community engagement, and leadership development. CLC Ayroor is known for its creative and impactful initiatives.

Follow on Instagram
Mathruvedi

The Mothers' Forum – A strong pillar of our parish community, Mathruvedi brings together the women of the parish for spiritual enrichment, mutual support, and charitable works. They lead many of the parish's social welfare initiatives.

Pithruvedi

The Fathers' Forum – Pithruvedi serves as a gathering of the men of the parish, fostering brotherhood, spiritual growth, and active participation in parish governance and development projects.

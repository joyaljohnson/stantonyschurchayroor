About Our Parish

St. Antony's Syro-Malabar Catholic Church, Ayroor, was established in 1925 when the foundation stone was laid by Fr. Joseph. Over the past century, this parish has grown from humble beginnings into a vibrant spiritual community. Situated along the Aluva-Mala Road, the church stands as a beacon of faith and hope for hundreds of families in the region.

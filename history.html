1925 – 2025

The Legacy of Faith

A journey through 100 years of divine grace

1925

Foundation Stone Laid

Fr. Joseph laid the foundation stone for St. Antony's Chapel in Ayroor, marking the beginning of a spiritual journey that would span a century. The faithful gathered with hope and devotion to establish a house of worship.

1950

Church Reconstruction

A grand reconstruction of the church was undertaken to accommodate the growing congregation. The new structure featured the iconic bell tower and architectural elements that became landmarks of the region.

1976

Independence as a Parish

Ayroor was declared an independent parish, a milestone that recognized the spiritual maturity and vibrancy of the community. The parish began to flourish with its own identity and leadership.

2025

Centenary Jubilee

The parish celebrates 100 glorious years of faith, community, and service. The Centenary Jubilee brings together generations of parishioners in a grand celebration of thanksgiving and hope for the future.
